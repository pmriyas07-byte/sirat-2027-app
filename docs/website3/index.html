Forum Overview

“A distinguished gathering. A defining global agenda.”

SIRAT Global Forum 2027 convenes the enterprise, influence and ambition of the global Tamil Nadu Muslim diaspora — business leaders, investors, innovators, professionals and emerging young leaders who are shaping industries, opening markets and advancing the community’s global presence.

Jointly organised by MUKMIN National Federation and the United Economic Forum, this three-day flagship gathering builds on its inaugural edition. Through influential dialogue, purposeful business engagement and an international trade show, it turns collective ambition into lasting economic opportunity across generations.
