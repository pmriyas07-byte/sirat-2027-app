Forum Overview

A distinguished gathering. A defining global agenda.

The SIRAT Global Forum 2027 brings together leading business figures, investors, innovators, professionals and emerging young leaders from the global Tamil Nadu Muslim diaspora — the people shaping industries, opening markets and advancing the community’s global presence.

Building on its inaugural edition, this three-day flagship gathering is jointly organised by MUKMIN National Federation and the United Economic Forum. It connects decision-makers and changemakers to forge strategic alliances, unlock cross-border trade and investment, and turn collective ambition into lasting economic opportunity.

Through influential dialogue, purposeful business engagement and an international trade show, SIRAT creates the setting for partnerships that matter — strengthening a globally connected diaspora business ecosystem, advancing future-ready leadership and ensuring economic success creates opportunity across generations.

SIRAT — Strengthening Indian Muslim Roots & Aspiration Together. Heritage and faith as a strong foundation for global progress, guided by integrity and shared prosperity.

Advance Education & Future Readiness

Build a future-ready talent pipeline through global partnerships, equipping communities with the skills, insights and networks to thrive.
